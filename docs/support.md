# Support

Search this guide before asking for help. Include the IPTVBoss version, operating system, relevant settings, and sanitized logs when you contact support.

## Choose a support path

- [Join the IPTVBoss Discord](https://discord.gg/s3kpjP8EgR) for community help, setup questions, and general troubleshooting.
- [Open a support ticket through the IPTVBoss member portal](https://members.bosstees.net/) for account, billing, private, or other issues that should not be discussed publicly.

!!! warning
    Never include playlist credentials, private URLs, tokens, license keys, or other secrets in a support ticket or screenshot.
